This curriculum will guide you through the process of developing a mobile app or web app that solves a problem in your community.

Are you an educator or youth-serving organization that wants to try out Technovation? Pilot our program with AI Mini Technovation.

Curriculum by Division


Beginner Division Curriculum

30+ hour curriculum for Technovation beginner division participants (ages 8-12) who want to submit a mobile or web app solution to a problem in their community. Topics covered are ideation, coding, AI, and pitching.


Junior Division Curriculum

50+ hour curriculum for Technovation junior division participants (ages 13-15) who want to submit a mobile or web app solution to a problem in their community. Topics covered are ideation, coding, AI, entrepreneurship, and pitching.


Senior Division Curriculum

50+ hour curriculum for Technovation senior division participants (ages 16-18) who want to submit a mobile or web app solution to a problem in their community. Topics covered are ideation, coding, AI, entrepreneurship, and pitching.


AI in Action Curriculum

50+ hour curriculum for all students who are interested in solving a real-world problem in their communities with AI.


Core Curriculum

A condensed version of the Technovation Girls curriculum containing the basic information needed to submit your project. We recommend using the full division curriculum courses if possible, but if you are short on time, the Core Curriculum is a useful tool.

Additional Pathways

These courses are alternate ways to work towards solving a problem for your submission. Most focus on particular problems or Sustainable Development Goals.

Technical Tutorials by SDG

This course includes tutorials showcasing some great Technovation alumnae app examples focused on particular SDGs. You will learn how the alumnae built a particular aspect of their app.



Technical Videos

This course consolidates all the technical videos available for coding and AI into one place.


Using Generative AI

This course contains tips and resources for using generative AI tools to help with your Technovation submission


Media Guide

This guide includes resources and information to get the word out about the amazing work you’ve done to create solutions to real problems that impact lives of people in your community.
