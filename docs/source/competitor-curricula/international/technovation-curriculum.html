Technovation Challenge

Best for: A semester, a full unit, or a club that wants to make something real.

Technovation Challenge takes students from ideation all the way to a working project. If they want to, they can enter it into our global competition.

What our students build

A 12-year-old from North Carolina built the Natural Disaster Rescue Assistant, a web app that creates personalized escape plans for emergencies like earthquakes, floods, and wildfires. Watching communities across the country get hit by disasters, they decided to make something useful. Using tools including mapping services and AI, she combined live data with clear guidance to make emergency response faster and easier to follow.
